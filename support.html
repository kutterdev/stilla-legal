Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Stilla, then Cancel Subscription. Your premium access stays active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device? Open Stilla, go to Profile, then tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you originally subscribed with.

Where is my data stored? On your device, and synced silently through your private iCloud account. We have no servers and cannot read your sessions, favorites, or journal.
