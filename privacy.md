# Stilla Privacy Policy

**Effective date: April 30, 2026**

Stilla is a meditation app built to respect your privacy. This page explains
what we do and don't collect.

## What we collect

**On your device only.** Your meditation history, favorite tracks, and mood
reflections are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's secure app
storage. They never leave your device unless you turn on iCloud sync (we'll
ask first when that feature ships).

**Subscription state.** When you start a free trial or subscribe, Apple
processes the purchase. We use RevenueCat to verify whether your
subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymized customer ID — no
name, no email, no payment details. Their privacy policy is at
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

**That's it.** We do not collect:
- Your name, email, phone number, or address
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
- Your browsing history or app usage analytics
- Any advertising identifiers

## Third parties

- **Apple** processes all payments. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **RevenueCat** handles subscription verification. See above.

We do not work with advertising networks, analytics providers, or data
brokers.

## Children

Stilla is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but we do not knowingly
collect any data about children. If you believe a child has provided
information through Stilla, contact us and we'll remove it.

## Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data, there's nothing on our servers to
delete. To clear your local data, delete the Stilla app from your device.
To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**
on your iPhone.

## Changes

If we update this policy we'll change the effective date above and post the
new version here. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
